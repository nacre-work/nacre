# Schedule 2 — data processing agreement

The customer is the controller. We are the processor and engage no
sub-processor without 30 days' notice and a right to object.

Sub-processors as of this version: the hosting provider, and nothing else. There
is no analytics vendor, no error-reporting service and no model provider — the
product runs inside the customer's own network, which is what makes this
schedule short.

On termination the customer may export within 30 days, after which we delete.
Deletion means the rows and the vectors, and it is complete within 14 days
including backups.
