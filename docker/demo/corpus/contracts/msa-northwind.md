# Master services agreement — Northwind Logistics

Contract number NW-2026-0431. Signed 14 March 2026, initial term 24 months,
auto-renewing for 12 unless either party gives 90 days' notice.

Service credits begin at 99.5% monthly availability, measured on the search
endpoint only. Below 99.0% the credit is 10% of the monthly fee; below 95.0% it
is 25% and the customer may terminate for cause.

Data processing is under the DPA attached as Schedule 2. The processing location
is the EU, and moving it requires written consent — this is the clause that
decides where the vector store may run, so read it before proposing an
architecture that spans regions.
