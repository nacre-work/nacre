# Leave and time off

Twenty-eight days of paid leave a year, plus public holidays in the country you
are employed from. Leave does not expire at the end of the year, but anything
above ten carried days needs your manager's sign-off in January.

Request leave at least two weeks ahead where you can. For anything longer than
ten working days, a month. This is not a rule about trust; it is a rule about
on-call rotation, which is built a month in advance and is painful to rebuild.

Sick leave is not counted against paid leave and needs no notice beyond telling
your team. After three consecutive days, HR needs a certificate for payroll.
