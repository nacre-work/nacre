# Expenses

Anything under 200 EUR that you would defend to a colleague, buy it and file the
receipt. Above that, ask first — not because the answer is likely to be no, but
because somebody may already have a licence.

Travel is booked through the company account. Economy on flights under six
hours, and anything above that is your call. Hotels up to 180 EUR a night in
western Europe, 120 elsewhere.

Receipts go into the expenses tool within thirty days. After ninety days
finance cannot reclaim the VAT, and the claim becomes a favour rather than a
process.
