# Onboarding

A new engineer gets accounts on day one and access to systems on day three.
The gap is deliberate: the access request goes through your manager, and the
manager is the person who knows which systems you actually need.

Laptops are issued by IT from the Vilnius office. If you are remote, the laptop
ships the week before your start date and arrives with disk encryption already
on. Do not disable it — a machine that holds customer data and is not encrypted
is a reportable incident under our data policy, not an inconvenience.

The first week has three fixed points: a walkthrough of the permission model on
Monday, an on-call shadowing session on Wednesday, and a review of the security
policy on Friday. Everything else is yours to arrange.
